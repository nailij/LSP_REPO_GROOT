# Question 4 – Personal Reflection on AI in Software Development

** 1.	Benefits: Explain how AI has helped your learning or software development this semester. Include at least one specific example from this course. **
- Use of AI has been beneficial throughout this semester because it ensures a high standard of work.  It provides a quicker a wider variety of test cases, including edge cases that may not otherwise be considered.  It also catches small syntax errors, that can significantly shorten the debugging phase.  In this class specifically, it aided in my transition to Java, as I am more used to coding in C++.

** 2.	Challenges: Describe a situation in which AI was incorrect, misleading, unhelpful, or made learning more difficult. Explain what you learned from that experience. If you did not encounter such a situation, explain a limitation or risk you have observed and how you address it. **
- While I don't have a specific example, AI can get stuck in a specific way of thinking when trying to solve a problem.  Even if that is not the correct pattern of thought, it will cycle through it until pulled out of that cycle.  For example, if you need addition and AI believes you require division, it may add subtraction and multiplication to the division, instead of simply trying addition.  This can be harder to identify with more complex problems, as the repititive thoughts may not be so obvious.  I've found when I am consistently failing to find a solution with AI, pointing out the previous failures, their causes, and offering new directions helps to reset it.

** 3.	Moving Forward: Explain how you intend to use AI in future courses or professional software development while continuing to develop your own technical skills. **
- I am currently working as a part-time Software Engineer at Marriott International.  I use AI to search through large amounts of code.  Many of my tickets require small changes in large files that are often not identified, so I have to search through 3 or 4 possible files, each with hundreds of lines, before I find my culprit.  AI helps to significantly speed up this process.  Additionally, I use it to help with planning and documentation.  When I start new projects, I also like to use AI to better help me understand the technical concepts needed to work through everything.
